Rekk

A free iPhone app for recovery and training.

Rekk turns your sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate into one daily recovery score, measured against your own baseline. A built-in workout log tracks your training. You can connect an Apple Watch through Apple Health or a Garmin device.

No subscription, no ads, and your data is never sold.
